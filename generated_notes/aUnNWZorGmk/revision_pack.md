## Interview Questions

### Q1: How do you handle situations where source material is unclear or incomplete during study?

**Answer:** I cross-reference with other reliable sources, re-watch the original lecture, or consult the instructor. I do not rely on ambiguous notes for critical concepts.

### Q2: What is your strategy for creating effective revision packs from lecture notes?

**Answer:** I extract key concepts, algorithms, and formulas, then summarize them into cheat sheets, flashcards, and quizzes. If content is unclear, I flag it for review rather than guessing.

### Q3: How do you ensure the accuracy of your study materials?

**Answer:** I verify facts against standard textbooks or peer-reviewed papers. For lecture notes, I check for consistency and clarity, and if unclear, I seek clarification from the source.

### Q4: Describe your approach to learning from video lectures.

**Answer:** I take active notes, pause to understand complex parts, and if a segment is unclear, I re-watch it or find supplementary material. I then consolidate notes into a structured revision format.

### Q5: What do you do if you encounter a gap in your knowledge while studying?

**Answer:** I identify the gap, research the topic using credible sources, and create targeted study materials to fill the gap. I ensure I understand the concept before moving on.
