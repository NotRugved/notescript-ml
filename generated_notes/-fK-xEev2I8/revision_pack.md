## Interview Questions

### Q1: What is the role of intuition in machine learning model development?

**Answer:** Intuition helps practitioners select appropriate algorithms, tune hyperparameters, and interpret model behavior by leveraging prior experience and heuristic understanding, complementing formal theory.

### Q2: Explain the difference between theoretical analysis and empirical intuition in ML.

**Answer:** Theoretical analysis provides formal guarantees and derivations based on assumptions, while empirical intuition relies on observed patterns, experiments, and heuristics; intuition guides model design when theory is insufficient or computationally infeasible.

### Q3: How would you address unclear content in a lecture when preparing revision notes?

**Answer:** Identify gaps, supplement with external resources, focus on key concepts highlighted, and use concise summaries to capture essential points despite ambiguity.

### Q4: What strategies can improve the clarity of theoretical sections in a Machine Learning lecture?

**Answer:** Use clear definitions, step-by-step derivations, visual aids, examples, and periodic checks for understanding to ensure the audience follows the theoretical development.

### Q5: Why is it important to have a structured revision pack like the one described?

**Answer:** A structured revision pack organizes information, facilitates active recall through flashcards, tests understanding with quizzes, and prepares candidates for technical interviews by covering both fundamentals and nuanced insights.
